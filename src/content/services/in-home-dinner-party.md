How often have you hosted a dinner party and spent the whole night in the kitchen, only to be exhausted by the time the last guest left? With BabyBlue, your next dinner party is relaxed from the first pour to the last bite.

It begins with a conversation in your home, where we design your menu together and suggest wines, beers and spirits to complement each course. On the day of your party we arrive early with everything we need. While you enjoy your guests, we do all the work. After the final course we quietly clean your kitchen, leaving you nothing to do but linger over coffee, tea and dessert.
